a notice to the newly washed-ashore

THE ISLAND RESETS.
WE REMEMBER.

Every few weeks the roads move. The mountains trade places. Your house — the one you swore you built to last — is gone by morning, and nobody talks about it.

But we were here before. And we will be here after.
We remember what you did at the airfield.
— the residents
