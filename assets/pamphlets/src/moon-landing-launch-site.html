JULY 1969 — "ONE SMALL STEP"

The moon footage was filmed
at Launch Site.

Compare the photographs. The scaffolding. The gantry. The big empty hangar with the suspiciously moon-colored floor. That rocket on the pad has been "about to launch" for as long as anyone can remember — because it only ever launched once, carrying nothing, filmed from the tower for the cameras. They never went. It was all shot right here, and then they abandoned the set and let the radiation keep the curious away.
